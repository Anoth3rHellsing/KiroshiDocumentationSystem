## 2025-05-23 - Optimizing String Similarity in Clustering
**Learning:** `difflib.SequenceMatcher` is computationally expensive (O(N*M)) compared to set-based Jaccard similarity. In clustering scenarios where strict thresholds are used, calculating a cheaper upper-bound score (based on Jaccard similarity) allows for aggressive pruning of candidates that cannot possibly meet the threshold, skipping the expensive `SequenceMatcher` call entirely.
**Action:** Implemented an early exit optimization in `_title_similarity_score` that returns 0.0 if `(0.6 + 0.4 * jaccard) < threshold`, and updated `_cluster_case_titles` to pass a dynamic `min_required` score. Benchmarks showed a ~1.7x speedup on a dataset of 700 titles.
