## 2025-05-24 - Optimization of Token Categorization
**Learning:** `Counter` construction from a generator expression (e.g., `Counter(x.lower() for x in tokens)`) is significantly slower than `Counter(tokens)` when `tokens` is already a list/tuple. Re-normalizing already normalized data in hot paths (like categorization loops) is a waste of CPU cycles.
**Action:** Removed redundant `lower()` calls and filtering in `_infer_report_category` and `_infer_structured_category`, relying on the contract that `_tokenize_issue_description` returns normalized tokens. This yielded ~19% speedup in synthetic benchmarks.
