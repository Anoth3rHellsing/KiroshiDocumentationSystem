# Refurbished Scanner FedEx Email Template (Second-Line Mode)

When a scanner or Move+ device is replaced under warranty with Trios Care enabled, send this e‑mail as soon as the FedEx tracking number is available.

```
Dear {customer_name} from {clinic_name},

I hope you are having an excellent day! This is {consultant_full_name} from 3Shape support regarding your case {case_number} about {issue_description}.

I am more than happy to inform you that we have created a ticket to send you a refurbished unit through FedEx which you can track by using the following tracking number: {fedex_tracking_number}.

Remember that this process will not have a cost.

Please also remember to send us back the faulty {device_type} using the shipping label you will find in the box. Please be informed that if we do not receive the faulty scanner within 32 days of your receipt of the new device, your TRIOS licenses will expire.

Wishing you the best again!
```
