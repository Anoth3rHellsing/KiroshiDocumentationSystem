## 2025-05-19 - Added Clear Filters button to Saved Cases
**Learning:** Users often get stuck in empty states when multiple filters (Search, Layout, Version, Legacy) are applied simultaneously. Providing a single "Clear Filters" action significantly reduces friction compared to manually resetting each filter.
**Action:** Always include a "reset" or "clear" action in empty states caused by filtering. This transforms a dead end into a recovery path.
