## 2025-05-19 - Missing Placeholders in Key Input Fields
**Learning:** Users often hesitate or enter data in inconsistent formats when critical identifiers (like Case ID or Subscription ID) lack specific examples. Providing a `placeholder` with a concrete example (e.g., 'e.g. CS-0012345') significantly reduces ambiguity and cognitive load.
**Action:** Audit all identifier-based inputs (IDs, serial numbers, codes) and ensure they include a `placeholder` attribute demonstrating the expected format.
